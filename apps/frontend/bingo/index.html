How to create an OSRS bingo board

  1. Name your event, choose the board dimensions and number of teams, and set separate admin and player passwords.
  2. Fill the tiles with your clan's goals. Add descriptions, OSRS item images and point values to suit the event.
  3. Share the board link and player password. Teams can record tile progress and attach proof text or screenshots while the board updates together.

Before the event

Can I choose my own bingo tasks?

Yes. You write the goals for your board, so you can mix boss drops, raids, skilling milestones and other challenges. The board starts with custom tiles for you to fill.

Do players need individual accounts?

Players join with the board name and player password. Admin access uses a separate password, and you can also require team passwords for progress changes.
