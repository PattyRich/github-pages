Boss and raid collections

Choose Phosani's Nightmare, Chambers of Xeric, Tombs of Amascut, Theatre of Blood, Corrupted Gauntlet, Yama or Nex. Each hunt keeps its own kill count, sessions and drop memories. For raids, record completed runs; for Corrupted Gauntlet, record successful corrupted completions.

Every 100 recorded kills or raids completes a window. Most collections have six windows; Corrupted Gauntlet has four. Completed windows stay in your sanctuary, and later completions begin another colour edition.

Starting your hunt

Does it record kills automatically?

You record each kill or raid in the tracker. KC Trackers is a manual journal: it does not read your RuneLite session or pull your in-game kill count.

Can I start with my existing KC?

Yes. Enter your starting KC as a baseline. It counts toward your total, while new kills recorded in the journal light the panes. Save drop labels and screenshots to their panes, and download postcards from your sessions.

Do I need an account?

You can start as a guest and save in this browser. An optional username and password account lets you open your saved hunts on another device. Export a backup to keep a copy of your KC, sessions, drop memories and window styles.
